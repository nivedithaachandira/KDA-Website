Founder's Message

Shaping the Future through Design

"Design has always been more than a profession to me - it is a passion, a purpose, and a powerful force for creating meaningful change."

Throughout my journey as a design educator, researcher, and practitioner, I have witnessed how design can inspire creativity, transform perspectives, solve complex challenges, and shape a better future.

Kannike Design Academy was born from a dream and a deep aspiration to contribute to the evolving landscape of design education. I strongly believe that learning should ignite curiosity, encourage exploration, challenge conventional thinking, and empower individuals to discover their creative potential.

Through KDA, I aspire to create a vibrant platform where educators, students, professionals, and institutions come together to learn, collaborate, innovate, and grow. More than an academy, KDA is a reflection of my commitment to pushing the boundaries of design education.
